# Changelog

What changed in each version, in plain words. The newest version is at the top.

## 0.1.0

The skeleton.

- Add customers and suppliers, and products and services, and edit them later.
- A starting chart of accounts, one tax rate and one stock location are set up the first time you open the app.
- Behind the scenes: invoices can be drafted, confirmed (numbered, with their sales, tax, stock and cost of sales posted to the books) and cancelled (every posting reversed). The screens for this come next.
- Everything is stored in this browser. There is no account and nothing is sent anywhere.
